# Credits for the demo video

## Music

- **Gymnopedie No. 1.** Piece by Erik Satie (1866 to 1925, public domain); this recording by Teknopazzo.
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Source: [Wikimedia Commons, File:Gymnopedie No. 1..ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gymnopedie_No._1..ogg).
- `music.ogg` is the first 92 seconds of that file, re-encoded (Ogg Vorbis). `compose.py` fades it in and out and mixes it low.

## Sounds

The soft UI sounds (select, send, done, copy, save, trash, restore, end card) are synthesized by `compose.py` from sine waves and filtered noise. No samples from third parties.

## Videos shown

Only public-domain or open-licensed videos: NASA ("Rocket Science in 60 Seconds", public domain) and the Blender Foundation open movies (Big Buck Bunny, Sintel, Tears of Steel, Spring, CC BY).
