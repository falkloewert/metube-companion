      Cookies

      Instagram and YouTube require a sign-in for some content. The browser extension sends your cookies with every request; you can also upload them manually here (Netscape format, cookies.txt). They apply to all downloads.

      Extension and help

      The extension sends the link of the open tab, including cookies, to this page. Chromium browsers: unzip the file, open the extensions page, enable developer mode, "Load unpacked". Firefox needs a Mozilla signature, see the project README.
